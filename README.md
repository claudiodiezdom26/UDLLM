# UDLLM
This project involves the development of a tool based on predictive models and artificial intelligence designed to identify patients at high risk of an unexpected hospital readmission within 30 days of discharge. Rather than functioning as a closed system that merely provides a numerical score, the tool analyzes a patient’s medical records and highlights the specific factors contributing to that risk—such as abnormal test results or particular medication combinations. This enables the medical team to take timely preventive action—for instance, by scheduling follow-up consultations or adjusting prescriptions—to safeguard the patient’s health and reduce excess hospital costs.
